The idea

A racing game, a road that writes itself, and a dream you can drive.

The game is a Mode-7 kart racer in the spirit of the 16-bit classics: drifts that charge mini-turbos, jump ramps that pay a trick boost, boost pads, item boxes, rocket starts, and up to seven rivals in three speed classes. Every sprite, texture and song is generated in code.

The circuit designer is a -parameter diffusion model that writes a lap as the 256 steps between its road points. It learned from laps drawn by a procedural architect, of them figure-eights, and it builds yours one arc at a time, steered by your driving.

The dream lab is a different model: a -parameter diffusion world model of an electric race car that renders every frame from the last four and your controls, live on your GPU. An audit checks its physics against the simulator it learned from.

Every model here was trained from scratch on one laptop, and runs in your browser.

The circuit designer

How a circuit is dreamed

Follow one figure-eight the designer built during an evaluation run, arc by arc, exactly the way the game builds the road during lap one.

  1. The grid comes first

    Before the countdown, the designer dreams the start straight and the first stretch: a quarter of the lap, with nothing to go on but the layout it was asked for.

  2. Then the road ahead, one arc at a time

    Each new arc is 32 road points, about 120 m of racing, inpainted around everything that already exists. The game keeps 28% of a lap dreamed ahead of the leader, so nobody ever reaches the edge.

  3. It reads how you drive

    Over the last eight seconds the race watches your speed, your drifting, time off the road and spins. Fast, sideways and clean asks for a wilder, more technical arc; off-road and bumping asks for a calmer one. The designer is conditioned on that style for every arc it draws.

  4. Every arc is checked before it becomes road

    The arc and the guess at the rest of the lap must pass the same rules as the training circuits: no corner tighter than the karts can take, no stretch too close to another, a straight start, clean crossings. If an arc fails, it is dreamed again.

  5. Crossings become bridges

    When a figure-eight crosses itself, the later stretch rises 6 m over the earlier one on 44 m ramps, with guard rails and pillars. Road that was already built is lifted after the fact.

  6. Then it locks

    When the leader closes the lap, the circuit is final: laps two and three run on exactly the road you watched appear.

Physics audit

Does the dream obey physics?

Video models produce plausible pixels; the question is whether they keep the laws of motion. The audit reads the dream's own frames back into numbers (ego-motion by differentiable image registration, speed from the dreamed speedometer) and checks them against the simulator that generated the training data, on circuits the model never saw.

Inside the model

The network grew a speedometer.

Linear probes on the 8×8 bottleneck recover the car's speed, yaw rate and the curvature of road it has not reached yet. Adding a probe's direction back into the activations causally changes the dream, the same intervention used to study world models in language models and RL agents.
